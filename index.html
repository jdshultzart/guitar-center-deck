The Story

From Customer

In 1981, JD Shultz walked into the original Hollywood Guitar Center on Sunset and started spending every dollar he had.

That store is where he built himself into a multi-instrumentalist, producer, mixer, and songwriter. More than four decades later, that relationship evolved into something no other artist has: JD's original artwork is now part of the Guitar Center environment.

A Relationship Built Over Years10 / 18
How It Started

A Relationship Built Over Years

It started in 2011. JD hung his first pieces inside Guitar Center, and the response was immediate. Customers stopped, looked, and asked about them. The art belonged there because it came from the same place the music does.

From there it grew on its own. One store became three. A handful of paintings became dozens. What began as a few canvases on a wall turned into wrapped guitars, featured walls, and a presence that customers now expect to see when they walk in.

More than a decade later, JD is the only artist with original work installed across multiple Guitar Center locations. This partnership is the next step in a story that's already been building for years.
